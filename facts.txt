
🐱 Fun fact: No cat is truly 100% hypoallergenic! Even hairless cats can trigger allergies because the main cat allergen, a protein called Fel d 1, is produced mainly in their saliva and skin glands, not just their fur.

Some breeds, such as Siberian cats and Balinese cats, are sometimes reported to produce less of this allergen, but it varies from cat to cat.

So, a cat with less fur doesn't necessarily mean fewer sneezes! 😸
